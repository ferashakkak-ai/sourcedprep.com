Is it right for you?

This is for you if you

  • qualify for 65/20 under the 2025 test: 65 or older at filing, with periods totaling at least 20 years as a permanent resident
  • find Spanish easier to read, and may take the civics test in your own language with an interpreter
  • want each English question with the Spanish beneath it, and every answer in both languages, in large print

This is not for you if you

  • read Spanish but do not qualify for 65/20: you study all 128 questions (see the free Spanish practice on this site)
  • filed before October 20, 2025: you take the 2008 test, and this 2025 list is not yours
  • read English comfortably and want the simpler English-only sheet (see the 65/20 sheet, 3.99 USD)

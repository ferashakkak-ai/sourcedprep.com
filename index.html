Texas Life, Accident and Health exam

A buyer takes delivery of a fixed annuity contract and soon changes her mind. What must the contract allow, under Texas law?

  1. ARescission for at least 10 days, with the insurer keeping any contract fees
  2. BRescission for at least 20 days, but only if the insurer agrees to the refund
  3. CRescission only through a surrender, with the insurer keeping any charges
  4. DRescission for at least 20 days, refunding premiums and any contract fees
Show the answer and its source

D. Rescission for at least 20 days, refunding premiums and any contract fees

A fixed annuity contract must give the purchaser at least 20 days after delivery to rescind and get back premiums paid, including contract fees or charges, with no conditions.

A refund that lets the insurer keep fees falls short of the statute, which covers premiums and any contract fees or charges.

Source: Tex. Ins. Code sec. 1116.002(a). From the 20 free Texas questions on this site. None of the free questions is in a kit.

Free look in California: 10 to 30 days, at least 30 for seniors

Last checked November 13, 2026.

After you receive a new individual life insurance policy or annuity in California, you can return it for cancellation during a "free look" (right to examine) period. How long that period lasts depends on who bought it and whether it replaced other coverage, and for small life policies, the face value.

SituationFree look periodSection
Individual life policy or individual annuity (general rule)Set by the insurer, at least 10 days and no more than 30 days after the owner receives itCal. Ins. Code 10127.9
Individual life policy with a face value under $10,00010 to 30 days, under its own sectionCal. Ins. Code 10127.7
Individual life policy or individual annuity sold to a senior (60 or older on the purchase date)At least 30 daysCal. Ins. Code 10127.10
A replacement of life insurance or an annuity30 days from delivery, for an unconditional refund of all premiums (variable products: account value plus any policy fee), when an agent sells the replacementCal. Ins. Code 10509.6(d)

What the owner gets back

  • Nonvariable life policies and annuities (including modified guaranteed contracts): returning the policy in time voids it from the start, as if it had never been issued. The insurer refunds all premiums and any policy fee within 30 days of learning of the cancellation. (10127.9(a)(2))
  • Variable life policies and variable annuities: the owner gets back the account value plus any policy fee, also within 30 days. (10127.9(a)(2); 10509.6(d) for replacements)
  • Seniors and variable products: during the 30 days the premium goes only into fixed-income investments and money-market funds, unless the owner directs it into the underlying funds. If the owner did not, a return voids the policy and all premiums and any policy fee are refunded. If the owner did, the refund is the account value plus any policy fee. In both cases the insurer refunds the premiums or the account value within 30 days after it learns of the cancellation. (10127.10(a)(1), (a)(2))

Where the notice goes

Under the general and senior rules, the right to return is printed on the front of the policy jacket or on the cover page, with the period clearly stated. (10127.9(a)(1), 10127.10(a)(1)) A policy under $10,000 may print it on or attach it to the policy (10127.7), and a replacing insurer may deliver it as a separate written document with the policy (10509.6(d)). For seniors, the notice is in 12-point bold type with one inch of space on all sides. (10127.10(c), (d))

What is not covered

The general, under-$10,000 and senior rules do not apply to life policies issued in connection with a credit transaction, or issued under a policy-change or conversion privilege in an existing policy. (10127.7, 10127.9(c), 10127.10(f))

Exam tip

"10 to 30 days" is the general range; "at least 30 days" is the senior rule; "60 or older" is the senior age for these free look rules. "30 days from delivery" is the replacement rule. The insurer's 30 days to pay a refund is a payment deadline, not a return period.

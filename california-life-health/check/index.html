About the exam

The exams are given by PSI. One kit, in final checks, will cover both the Life and the Accident and Health exams. The Life and the Accident and Health exams each have 75 questions; the combined exam has 150. The PSI candidate bulletin (revised October 2026) lists 105 minutes for each single exam and 195 minutes for the combined exam; the Department of Insurance's examination objectives (effective January 1, 2026) state 90 minutes for each 75-question exam. Check the current bulletin before your test date. These 20 free questions are a set of their own.

How downloads work

How a download works

Every Sourced Prep product is a PDF you download once and keep. There is no account and no subscription. When the store opens, checkout and receipts are handled by Gumroad, the merchant of record, and the download link arrives on the page after payment and in the receipt email.

Printing

The files are US Letter size. Print at actual size (100 percent), not "fit to page". Flashcards print double-sided, flipping on the long edge; each flashcard file also explains a fold-in-half method for printers that cannot print on both sides.

Reading on a screen

The kits read best on a tablet or computer. On a phone, zoom in, or print the pages you need.

If something goes wrong

If a file does not open, is not as described, or you bought it twice by mistake, write to hello@sourcedprep.com within 30 days: see the refund policy.

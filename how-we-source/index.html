How we source our answers

The rules every Sourced Prep file is written and checked by.

Every answer names its source

Each practice answer names the place it rests on: a section of a statute or regulation, a government consumer page, or a public government list such as the USCIS civics questions. You can open the source and read it yourself. Codes and standards that are not free to read are never quoted; where a kit relies on one, it names the section and puts the point in its own words.

Sources are fetched, read and dated

A source is used only after its text has been fetched from the government or official publisher and read. Each kit, each free question set and each question list says when its sources were last checked. When a law or a list changes, the product is checked again and the date moves.

The questions are written, not copied

The insurance practice questions are original. They are written from the source text and the published exam outlines. They are not questions from any exam, and no study material, including ours, can guarantee a result. The citizenship questions are USCIS's own public list, which is free at uscis.gov: our citizenship products add the format (cards, large print, Spanish help), never the questions.

Checked in a separate pass

Every answer is checked against the source text in a separate review pass before a file is signed off, and each signed file is recorded with its checksum, so the file you buy is the file that was checked.

Every product says who it is not for

Each product page says who it is for and who it is not for, and each kit says what it leaves out, so you can tell before you buy whether it fits your exam. Where another product, or a free page, suits you better, the page says so.

Free questions are a separate set

The free practice questions on this site are a set of their own. None of them is in a paid kit, so trying them tells you how we write without giving away a kit.

Corrections are posted

If you find an error, write to hello@sourcedprep.com with the page and the source you rely on. Every report is checked against the source text, and confirmed corrections are posted on the corrections page.

What we never claim

We never say a product is official or approved by an exam body or agency, never promise a pass, never call our questions exam questions, and never show reviews or numbers we cannot back. Sourced Prep is independent of USCIS, every state insurance department and every testing company.

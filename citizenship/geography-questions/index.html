Geography on the civics test: only on the 2008 list

Last checked November 13, 2026.

If you filed Form N-400 before October 20, 2025, you take the 2008 civics test, and its list has a geography section. The 2025 list has no geography section: it does not ask you to name an ocean, a river, a state on the Canada or Mexico border, or one of the five U.S. territories. Not sure which test you take? See 2025 or 2008 civics test.

The 2008 geography questions

2008 questionAnswers USCIS accepts
88. Name one of the two longest rivers in the United States.Missouri (River); Mississippi (River)
89. What ocean is on the West Coast of the United States?Pacific (Ocean)
90. What ocean is on the East Coast of the United States?Atlantic (Ocean)
91. Name one U.S. territory.Puerto Rico; U.S. Virgin Islands; American Samoa; Northern Mariana Islands; Guam
92. Name one state that borders Canada.Maine; New Hampshire; Vermont; New York; Pennsylvania; Ohio; Michigan; Minnesota; North Dakota; Montana; Idaho; Washington; Alaska
93. Name one state that borders Mexico.California; Arizona; New Mexico; Texas
94. What is the capital of the United States? 65/20Washington, D.C.
95. Where is the Statue of Liberty? 65/20New York (Harbor); Liberty Island. Also accepted: New Jersey, near New York City, and on the Hudson (River)

65/20 Were you 65 or older when you filed, with at least 20 years in total as a permanent resident? Then you study only the 20 questions marked 65/20. See the 65/20 rule.

If the 65/20 rule applies to you, questions 94 and 95 are the only geography questions in your 20.

Memory help

  • Only four states touch Mexico: California, Arizona, New Mexico and Texas. Learning one is enough, for example your own state if it is on the list.
  • The Pacific is on the West Coast. The Atlantic is on the East Coast.
  • The Mississippi runs north to south through the middle of the country. The Missouri joins it.

On the 2025 test

Two of these answers are still on the 2025 list: the capital of the United States (question 119) and where the Statue of Liberty is (question 120).

Practice the 2008 list, the 2025 list or a practice interview.

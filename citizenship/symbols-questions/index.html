The flag, the anthem and the Statue of Liberty on the civics test

Last checked 13 November 2026.

Six questions on the 2025 test are about American symbols. The answers are short.

These are 2025 test questions. You take the 2025 test if you filed Form N-400 on or after October 20, 2025. See 2025 or 2008 civics test.

Each line that starts with "or" is another answer USCIS accepts.

QuestionAnswers USCIS accepts
119. What is the capital of the United States?Washington, D.C.
120. Where is the Statue of Liberty?

New York (Harbor)

or Liberty Island

or New Jersey

or near New York City

or on the Hudson (River)

121. Why does the flag have 13 stripes? 65/20

(Because there were) 13 original colonies

or (Because the stripes) represent the original colonies

122. Why does the flag have 50 stars?

(Because there is) one star for each state

or (Because) each star represents a state

or (Because there are) 50 states

123. What is the name of the national anthem?The Star-Spangled Banner
124. The Nation's first motto was "E Pluribus Unum." What does that mean?

Out of many, one

or We all become one

65/20 Were you 65 or older when you filed, with at least 20 years in total as a permanent resident? Then you study only the 20 questions marked 65/20. See the 65/20 rule.

What the study guide adds

  • The flag: red stands for bravery, white for purity and blue for justice. The 13 red and white stripes are the 13 original colonies, and the 50 stars are the 50 states.
  • The anthem: Francis Scott Key wrote "The Star-Spangled Banner" about seeing the flag after a battle in the War of 1812. "Banner" means flag.
  • The Statue of Liberty: France gave it to the United States in 1886, as a symbol of friendship, freedom and democracy. She holds a torch in her right hand and a tablet in her left. The tablet shows July 4, 1776, the day the Declaration of Independence was officially adopted.
  • The Pledge of Allegiance: at the naturalization ceremony, everyone is asked to stand, face the flag, put the right hand over the heart and say the Pledge.

Practice with all 128 questions of the 2025 test or a practice interview. For the capital and the Statue of Liberty on the 2008 test, see geography on the civics test.

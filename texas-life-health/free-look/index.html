Free look and right to return in Texas: 20 days and 30 days

Last checked 13 November 2026.

A "free look" is a period after you receive a policy or contract during which you can return it for a refund. In Texas the length depends on the product. Learn each number with the product it belongs to.

Product or situationPeriodRule
Annuity (fixed, variable or modified guaranteed, individually solicited)At least 20 days after delivery to rescind the contract. A fixed annuity refunds all premiums, including contract fees and charges. A variable or modified guaranteed annuity refunds the cash surrender value plus fees and charges deducted, except where the buyer is an accredited investor.Tex. Ins. Code 1116.002(a), (b), (c)
Medicare supplement plan30 days after delivery, for a refund of the premium, if not satisfied for any reasonTex. Ins. Code 1652.155(a)
Long-term care benefit plan30 days after delivery, for a refund of any premium, if not satisfied for any reasonTex. Ins. Code 1651.054(c)
Replacement of life insurance or an annuity30 days after delivery, for an unconditional full refundTex. Ins. Code 1114.053(e)

Notices

  • Annuity: the 20-day right must be written into the contract itself. (1116.002(a), (b))
  • Medicare supplement: the plan or certificate must carry a notice of the return right, printed prominently on its first page or attached to the plan or certificate. The issuer must pay the refund straight to the applicant, without delay. (1652.155(b), (c))
  • Long-term care: the plan must carry a notice of the return right, printed prominently on its first page or attached to the plan document. The issuer pays the refund, in a timely manner, to whoever paid the premium. (1651.054(b), (d))
  • Replacement: the replacing insurer gives the owner notice of the 30-day right. (1114.053(e)) See replacement rules.

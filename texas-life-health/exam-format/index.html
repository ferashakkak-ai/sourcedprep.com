The Texas Life and Health exam: format, time, fee and passing score

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Pearson VUE publishes a candidate handbook and content outlines for the Texas insurance licensing exams. Here is how the life and health exams are built, from those two publications.

Two exams, two licenses

Life AgentGeneral Lines: Life, Accident and Health
Exam codeInsTX-Life01 (Spanish: InsTX-Life21)InsTX-LAH05 (Spanish: InsTX-LAH25)
Time allowed120 minutes150 minutes
Fee39 USD49 USD
General part50 scored questions plus 5 pretest100 scored questions plus 10 pretest
Texas part30 scored questions plus 5 pretest30 scored questions plus 5 pretest

(Pearson VUE Texas candidate handbook, publication 124400; Texas content outlines, publication 124401, effective 1 September 2026.)

Passing score

You need a scaled score of 70, on a scale of 0 to 100. The score is for the exam as a whole, not for each part separately. (Handbook 124400.)

Pretest questions

Each part also carries pretest questions on top of the scored ones, as the table shows.

Which license do you need?

The Texas Department of Insurance (TDI) lists separate application steps for the Life Agent license and the General Lines Life, Accident, Health and HMO license on its agent pages. Check the TDI page for the license your employer or agency asks for before you book the exam.

After you are licensed

A General Lines LAH license needs 24 hours of continuing education in each two-year renewal period, including 3 hours of ethics. (TDI continuing education page.)

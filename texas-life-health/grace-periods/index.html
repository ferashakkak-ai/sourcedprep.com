Grace periods in Texas life and health policies

Last checked 13 November 2026.

A grace period is the time after a premium is due during which you can still pay and the policy stays in force. The Texas Insurance Code sets minimums for each kind of policy, and the minimums differ by policy type.

PolicyMinimum grace periodSection
Individual lifeAt least one month, for every premium after the firstTex. Ins. Code 1101.005(a)
Group life31 days, for every premium after the firstTex. Ins. Code 1131.103(a)
Individual accident and health, weekly premiumAt least 7 daysTex. Ins. Code 1201.209(b)
Individual accident and health, monthly premiumAt least 10 daysTex. Ins. Code 1201.209(b)
Individual accident and health, any other premium modeAt least 31 daysTex. Ins. Code 1201.209(b)

The commissioner may by rule require a longer grace period for certain life policies. (Tex. Ins. Code 1101.005(b))

What happens during the grace period

  • Life: the policy stays in force. The policy may provide for an interest charge on a premium paid during the grace period. If the insured dies during the grace period, the policy may provide that the overdue premium is deducted from any settlement under the policy. (1101.005(a))
  • Group life: the death benefit coverage continues unless the policyholder or premium payor gives the insurer written notice of discontinuance, before the date of discontinuance and as the policy requires. The policy may make the policyholder or premium payor liable for a pro rata premium for the time the policy was in force during the grace period. (1131.103(a), (b))
  • Accident and health: the policy continues in force during the grace period, which applies to each premium after the first. A policy with a cancellation provision may make this subject to the insurer's right to cancel. (1201.209(a), (c))

Exam tip

Note which policy the question asks about, and whether the premium is weekly, monthly or another mode. The minimums differ.
